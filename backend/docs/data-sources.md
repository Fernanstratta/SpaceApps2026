# Data sources and provenance

| Provider or source | How it reaches the API | Transformations, update cadence, and gaps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NASA Science APOD Basic feed | Runtime HTTPS request from `/api/apod` | Selects the newest usable dated entry, strips HTML from display text, and retains NASA article links and credit. Video or incompatible images produce `imageUrl: null`. Timeout is four seconds. Upstream failure returns the archived Blue Marble entry. Shared caches are 15 minutes for NASA and one minute for fallback. [NASA feed](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-json/wp/v2/apod-basic) · [Archive credit](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2007-march-25-welcome-to-planet-earth/) |
| NASA Science planet facts | Bundled summaries and citations in `/api/planets` | Eight editorial records serve in orbital order with a 24-hour shared cache. The live provider is not contacted for these requests. Keep each card’s `sourceUrl`. [NASA Solar System](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/) |
| NASA POWER | Offline daily-point ingestion for 2024, surfaced in Earth `measurements` via `/api/locations` and `/api/compare` | `T2M` is two-meter air temperature. The script selects a 0.5° × 0.625° meteorology grid cell at each marker, filters fill and invalid values, checks Celsius units, and stores a mean in `degC` rounded to two decimals. Records are marked `modeled` and include valid-day counts, footprint, and scope. A failed refresh logs unavailability and leaves any existing record in place. [POWER](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/) · [Ingestion log](../research/power-ingestion.json) |
| Curated regional evidence | Versioned catalog records in `/api/analogs`, `/api/compare`, `/api/sources`, and `/api/datasets` | Smithsonian GVP, published papers, NASA and USGS sources, and discovery portals are cataloged by ID. Field-level provenance includes status, limitations, and, where available, resolution, temporal coverage, and license. Dataset and portal links point to the provider. Records change when the bundled catalog is updated and deployed. [Source catalog](../data/scientific-sources.json) · [Research findings](../research/baja-california-planetary-analogs.md) |
| Regional satellite and planetary imagery | Bundled metadata in `/api/imagery` and `/api/compare` | Records include source page, product, coordinate body, credit and licensing. `previewUrl`, acquisition time, and spatial resolution can be null. Use the source link for discovery records. Preset demo visuals have additional provenance in the dataset guide. [Imagery catalog](../data/satellite-imagery.json) · [Dataset guide](dataset-access.md) |
| Published crater dimensions and other lunar evidence | Offline site and measurement records bundled in `/api/lunar-analogs` | The ILS converts the published El Elegante/Linné dimensions to d/D and reports a score and weighted coverage. The detail includes source, method, and status for populated sides. Planned Copernicus, NASA, and other inputs are shown in `source_plan`. Unavailable sides are null and placeholders are excluded from scoring. The generated catalog changes when rebuilt and deployed. [Measurement records](../pipeline/catalog/measurements.json) · [Nine axes](comparison-axes.md) |

Earth markers use WGS84 coordinates. Regional Moon and Mars markers carry the body’s coordinate metadata. Lunar ILS records provide east-positive geographic degrees and an area-of-interest radius in meters. Keep each body on its own map and preserve coordinate and footprint warnings.

Keep source titles, original links, image credits, and the catalog’s attribution text alongside exported or displayed data. Source and imagery records carry item-specific license fields. Regional rankings and lunar ILS scores are calculated heuristics. NASA POWER summaries are modeled context, and educational scenarios are labeled in the returned records.
